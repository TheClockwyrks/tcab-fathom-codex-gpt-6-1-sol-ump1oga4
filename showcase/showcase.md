# Fathom

Carry a little light into a very deep dark. Fathom is a maze chase beneath the ocean, where glowing plankton trace flooded passages and every amber glimmer asks you to take a chance.

Your first corridor is a small pocket of certainty. Eat to brighten your light and widen your view. Send sonar around corners to remember the way ahead, then thread those half-seen routes toward the last mote. Clear the maze and descend: the next depth brings more hunters and a shorter sonar reach.

Three predators listen to different signals. The Lanternjaw follows your glow, wearing the same amber disguise as a harmless drifter. The Gloamfin hears through rock and answers your sonar with its own. The Flarefish lights up entire chambers, turning a welcome glimpse of the maze into a dangerous invitation.

Hold a corner, cast a pulse, or leave ink behind you. Your map remembers, but the dark returns beyond your circle. Staying alive means knowing when to shine—and when to slip away.
